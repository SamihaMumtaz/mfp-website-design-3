Our Leadership

"From the desk of CEO: The man behind the success of Maqsood Faisal Printing Press was the enlightenment of Chairman Haji Maqsood Ahmed (Late). His Dedication, Devotion & Commitment has forced us to keep this torch as the source of inspiration to move ahead."

Integration, innovation, and Specialty for value addition are the main criteria, we are adhering to. "Emerging Quality through design" is the Philosophy to maintain quality and customer satisfaction. Qualification for the certification of ISO 9001:2015 & FSSC 22000 version 5.1 in a row enabled the company to be recognized Internationally. The customer-driven strategy has attracted several diversified clienteles to tie in the business loop.

In our approach, a diversified model of business is the key parameter to entail success. We believe in customer relationships and customer satisfaction by keeping no elusiveness in business deals.

To gear up the momentum of the gained success, the company is striving to an expansion in Allama Iqbal Industrial Estate – SEZ of CPEC. Blown Film Extrusion is the new project to explore along with Tissue Paper Convertor.

Research & Development, training of the employees and meeting the regulatory compliance is the key components we are focusing followed by strict discipline and SOPs.
